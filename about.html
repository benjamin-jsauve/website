About

I study Mathematics and Computer Science at McGill in the Honours stream. My academic focus is at the intersection of probability, optimization, and machine learning, which maps pretty naturally onto the problems I find interesting in finance.

I spent time at my internships doing quantitative research and software development. Both gave me different angles on how research ideas actually make it into production, which I think about a lot.

Before any of this, I worked as a professional chef in some of the better kitchens in Canada.

I train MMA and play hockey. I ski when I get the chance. I read philosophy mostly for pleasure, Camus and Wittgenstein in particular. I’m serious about music in a way that’s hard to explain briefly.
